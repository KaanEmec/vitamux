Epic: [E06 Jobs, scheduling and sync state](README.md) · Status: todo · Depends on: J06.1
Read: [reliability#upgrades](../../architecture/reliability.md#upgrades)

**Objective:** Recover stuck work and make upgrades safe.

## Outputs
- Reaper loop
- SIGTERM drain: stop claiming, finish or checkpoint, release leases

## Done when
- A job killed with `kill -9` is re-claimed after lease expiry and resumes from its checkpoint.
- SIGTERM completes or releases within the grace period.
