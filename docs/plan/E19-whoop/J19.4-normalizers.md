# J19.4 Go normalizers and catalogue codes

Epic: [E19 WHOOP connector](README.md) · Status: in progress · Depends on: J19.1, J07.4
Read: [connectors#normalizer-contract](../../architecture/connectors.md#normalizer-contract), [metric-catalog](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md), [metric-catalog#provider-namespaced-scores](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md#provider-namespaced-scores), [resolution#sleep-episode-alignment](../../architecture/resolution.md#sleep-episode-alignment)

**Objective:** Turn WHOOP raw payloads into canonical records without losing granularity.

## Outputs
- `internal/connectors/whoop` normalizers, one per stream family, pure and golden-tested:
  - **Samples:** HR at its native step; steps as intervals.
  - **Cycles:** strain and recovery as provider-scoped daily values. Resting HR, `hrv_rmssd_nightly`, SpO2, skin temperature and respiration go to their shared codes when J19.1 confirms the definitions match.
  - **Sleep:** sessions with stages, naps kept separate.
  - **Workouts:** with sport, strain, HR zones and weightlifting detail.
  - **Time:** local dates in the user's timezone, from WHOOP's offsets. A cycle is not a local day; its value is dated by the rule in J19.1.
  - **Device:** `device_type` band.
- Provider-scoped catalogue codes (`whoop_recovery`, `whoop_strain`, `whoop_sleep_performance`, others confirmed in J19.1) in a catalogue migration and [metric-catalog](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md); `metrics.md` regenerated.
- Shape fingerprints that report missing or retyped required fields as drift.

## Done when
- Goldens cover each normalized stream, a rescored sleep, a nap, a workout with weightlifting detail, a cycle that crosses midnight, an unknown field (warning) and a missing required field (drift).
- On the synthetic dataset, WHOOP 6-second HR does not outweigh a sparser source inside a bucket (within-source aggregation first), and WHOOP sleep aligns with other sources' episodes.
