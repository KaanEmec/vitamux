# J25.6 Garmin cold-storage reload (opt-in)

Epic: [E25 Catalogue completeness and mapping corrections](README.md) · Status: done · Depends on: J25.5
Read: [providers/garmin](../../providers/garmin.md), [connectors#runtime-responsibilities](../../architecture/connectors.md#runtime-responsibilities)

**Objective:** An owner can recover intraday history Garmin has moved to cold storage, slowly and within Garmin's limits.

Garmin keeps intraday detail online for a few months; older days answer with empty series while daily summaries stay. A reload request (`POST /wellness-service/wellness/epoch/request/{date}`) restores a day for about a week, and is refused after roughly 30 requests a day (community findings, recorded in the provider page).

## Outputs
- Sidecar endpoint that requests a reload for one date and reports `SUBMITTED` or `DENIED`.
- A slow backfill mode: off by default, started from the Garmin connection page for a chosen range; a daily budget below Garmin's limit, then a refetch of the reloaded days' intraday streams.
- Progress and a clear stop when Garmin denies; never retried in a tight loop.

## Done when
- Fake-sidecar test: a range is reloaded within the budget, refetched and normalized; a denial pauses until the next day.
