# J25.3 Withings activity, intraday and sleep streams

Epic: [E25 Catalogue completeness and mapping corrections](README.md) · Status: done (2026-10-05) · Depends on: J25.1, J25.2
Read: [providers/withings](../../providers/withings.md), [connectors#withings-connector-reference-pattern](../../architecture/connectors.md#withings-connector-reference-pattern)

**Objective:** Withings activity, intraday and sleep data reach the catalogue. Replaces [J08.6](../E08-withings/J08.6-activity-sleep.md).

## Outputs
- `withings.activity` (`getactivity`): steps, distance, `elevation` (Withings: floors climbed) → `floors_climbed`, `calories` → `active_energy`, `totalcalories` → `total_energy`, soft/moderate/intense → intensity times; HR summary and zones raw. Rows with `brand` 18 are relays from other apps.
- `withings.intraday` (`getintradayactivity`): steps, elevation, calories, distance as intervals; heart rate, SpO2, respiratory rate as samples; few-second RMSSD and 1-min SDNN under a method context or their own codes; core body temperature estimate under its own code.
- `withings.sleep` (`sleep v2 get` and `getsummary`): states 0 awake, 1 light, 2 deep, 3 rem, 4 manual (unspecified, manual flag), 5 unspecified, 15 out of bed; summary efficiency (ratio × 100), latency, WASO, wake-up count → `sleep_awakenings`, snoring, apnea-hypopnea index, `withings_sleep_score`, breathing quality as `withings_breathing_quality`.
- Scopes and setup copy for the extra data; `docs/providers/withings.md` updated.

## Done when
