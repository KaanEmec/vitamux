# J25.5 Garmin mappings and new streams

Epic: [E25 Catalogue completeness and mapping corrections](README.md) · Status: todo · Depends on: J25.1
Read: [providers/garmin](../../providers/garmin.md), [ADR-0018](../../adr/0018-garmin-upstream.md)

**Objective:** Garmin SpO2 and every other Garmin value reach the catalogue.

## Outputs
- **SpO2:** the per-minute readings are in the sleep payload (`wellnessEpochSPO2DataDTOList`, 60 s, with reading confidence); `daily/spo2` carries summaries and hour-aligned averages, and its spot lists are usually null. The sleep normalizer maps the per-minute readings to `spo2` samples (stored raws re-normalize, no refetch) and `avgSleepSpO2` to `spo2_nightly`; spot values stay mapped; hourly averages stay raw (aggregates).
- **Daily summary:** `totalKilocalories` → `total_energy`; moderate and vigorous intensity minutes → intensity times; `sedentarySeconds` → `sedentary_time`; body battery charged and drained → provider-scoped codes. Stress, heart-rate and SpO2 summaries are derivable and stay raw with that reason.
- **Sleep:** `awakeCount` → `sleep_awakenings`; `avgSkinTempDeviationC` → `sleep_temperature_deviation`; stage −1 → `unknown`. The sleep heart-rate, stress, body-battery, HRV and respiration arrays duplicate all-day streams and stay raw.
- **New sidecar streams:** floors (15-min intervals, ascended → `floors_climbed`), hydration (`diet_water`), fitness age (`garmin_fitness_age`), training status (acute and chronic load; status in context). Training readiness keeps `inputContext` in context; body composition `bodyWater` → `body_water_ratio`.
- Verified sample intervals (heart rate 2 min, stress and body battery 3 min, respiration 2 min, steps and floors 15 min, HRV 5 min, sleep SpO2 1 min) and the cold-storage note replace the "unverified" lines in the provider page; the `events.go` comment about sleep vitals is corrected.

## Done when
- Goldens for each changed or new stream; sidecar tests for the new endpoints; reprocessing a stored sleep raw yields SpO2 samples.
