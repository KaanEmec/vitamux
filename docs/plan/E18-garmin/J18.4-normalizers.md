# J18.4 Go normalizers and catalogue codes

Epic: [E18 Garmin Connect connector](README.md) · Status: in progress · Depends on: J18.1, J07.4
Read: [connectors#normalizer-contract](../../architecture/connectors.md#normalizer-contract), [metric-catalog](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md), [metric-catalog#provider-namespaced-scores](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md#provider-namespaced-scores), [data-model#tables](../../architecture/data-model.md#tables)

**Objective:** Turn Garmin raw payloads into canonical records without losing granularity.

## Outputs
- `internal/connectors/garmin` normalizers, one per stream family, pure and golden-tested:
  - **Samples:** HR, stress, Body Battery, SpO2, respiration as samples at their native interval.
  - **Steps:** 15-minute intervals and the provider daily total as separate kinds; totals are never added to intervals.
  - **Nightly values:** resting HR, `hrv_rmssd_nightly`, plus HRV samples where Garmin provides them.
  - **Sleep:** sessions with stages and naps.
  - **Workouts:** from activity JSON, with laps as segments. FIT files stay raw; decoding them is later work.
  - **Groups:** body composition and BP readings.
  - **Training:** VO2max and training metrics.
  - **Devices:** from Garmin's device list (`device_type` watch, band, scale, BP monitor).
  - **Time:** local dates in the user's timezone, using Garmin's offsets.
- Provider-scoped catalogue codes (`garmin_body_battery`, `garmin_stress`, `garmin_training_readiness`, `garmin_sleep_score`, others confirmed in J18.1) in a catalogue migration and [metric-catalog](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md); `metrics.md` regenerated.
- Shape fingerprints that report missing or retyped required fields as drift.

## Done when
- Goldens cover each stream, a revised day, a split night and nap, an activity with laps, a manual BP entry, an unknown field (warning) and a missing required field (drift).
- On the synthetic dataset, `builtin:steps` never sums a Garmin daily total with its intervals, and `garmin` beats `garmin_apple` where both exist.
