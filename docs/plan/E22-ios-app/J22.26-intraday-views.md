# J22.26 Intraday views: one day at the metric's own resolution

Epic: [E22 Vitamux iOS app](README.md) · Status: done (2026-10-05) · Depends on: J22.6, J22.8, [E26](../E26-intraday-views/README.md) (catalogue, engine, API and panel parts, with research adjustments to the ladder below); this job keeps the app Day view (T22.26.3)
Read: [resolution#windows](../../architecture/resolution.md#windows), [metric-catalog#rules](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md#rules), [api#owner-endpoints-apiv1](../../architecture/api.md#owner-endpoints-apiv1), [ios-app#charts](../../architecture/ios-app.md#charts)

**Objective:** A metric can be looked at inside a day at the resolution that suits it: heart rate down to 30-second buckets and the raw readings, steps as 30-minute bars, and nothing finer than a day for metrics measured once. The resolution rules live in the catalogue, so both clients draw the same thing without per-metric code.

Owner decision (2026-10-04): some metrics need a much more granular view than a day; heart rate at about 30 s, steps at about 30 min.

## Resolution ladder

Decided per catalogue aggregation; `intraday` metadata on each metric records its default and finest bucket. The client picks the bucket from the visible span, and the user can pinch or zoom through the ladder.

| Aggregation | Metrics (examples) | Default bucket (24 h view) | Zoomed in | Finest | Drawn as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `intensive`, high-frequency | `heart_rate`, `spo2`, `respiratory_rate`, `blood_glucose`, `skin_temperature`, `garmin_stress`, `garmin_body_battery`, running and cycling speed, power, cadence | 1 min | 6 h → 1 min · 1 h → 30 s | raw samples (≤ 15 min span) | line, min–max band per bucket, source series |
| `intensive`, sparse | `hrv_sdnn`, `hrv_rmssd`, walking asymmetry, step length, double support | 5 min | 1 h → 1 min | raw samples | dots joined when ≤ 15 min apart |
| `additive` | `steps`, `distance_*`, `active_energy`, `basal_energy`, `floors_climbed`, `exercise_time`, `stand_time`, `diet_*` | 30 min | 6 h → 15 min · 1 h → 5 min | 1 min | bars; intervals pro-rated into buckets |
| `daily_summary`, `latest`, `sleep_derived` | `resting_heart_rate`, `weight`, `vo2max`, `bp_*`, body composition, `sleep_*` | none | — | — | no intraday view; readings or the night view instead |
| events | `health_events` codes | — | — | — | the existing timeline lanes, scrollable to an hour |

Overlays on every intraday chart: night shading from the resolved sleep episode, workout bands from `/resolved/workouts` tinted in the metric hue, a "now" line on today, and the coverage strip per source. Tapping a bucket shows value, bucket span, source and the per-bucket explanation; a raw sample shows its exact timestamp, device and origin.

## Outputs
- **Catalogue:** `intraday {default, finest}` on every metric (`internal/catalog`), with the ladder above as the rule; the generated [metrics.md](../../metrics.md) and `GET /metrics` expose it. A metric without `intraday` has no intraday view.
- **Engine:** `bucket(30s)` joins the bucket sizes (1, 5, 15, 30 min) for intensive metrics; rules may name it; the cache still skips bucket windows. [resolution](../../architecture/resolution.md) windows table updated.
- **API:** `GET /resolved/series?window=bucket:30s|1m|5m|15m|30m|hour` returns per bucket the value, status, `source`, and `min`/`max`/`n` so the band and decimation need no second call; capped to 1 day for ≤ 1 min buckets and 7 days for ≥ 5 min. `GET /sources/series` gains `grain=30s|1m|5m|15m|raw` for spans ≤ 1 day, read from `measurements` (the hourly aggregates stay for `hour|day`). `raw` pages samples with `cursor`.
- **App:** a **Day** tab on the range picker of every metric with `intraday`, with a date stepper, pinch and drag to zoom through the ladder, the overlays, the point sheet, and a "Show as table" fallback. Tapping a day on the weekly or monthly chart drills into it. Sleep-related metrics open the Day view centred on the night.
- **Panel:** the same Day range and chart in `/explore/[metric]`, built with the E21 kit; the all-sources day drilldown reuses it.
- **Grammar fixture:** cases for the default bucket per aggregation and for a metric without `intraday`.
- **Performance:** a WHOOP day of 6-second heart rate (14,400 rows) plus an Apple Watch day resolves to 1-minute buckets in under 300 ms on the synthetic dataset; raw spans stay under 2,000 points per source per request.

## Done when
- Every metric with `intraday` in the synthetic dataset opens the Day view in both clients; metrics without it show no Day tab.
- Zooming from 24 h to 15 min on heart rate passes through 1 min, 30 s and raw, with the band, source series, night and workout overlays correct; steps show 30-minute bars that sum to the day's resolved total within pro-rating.
- A bucket's explanation names the same inputs as the all-sources view for that span.
- Performance budgets above hold; UI tests and Playwright specs cover the Day view, zoom levels and the table fallback.
