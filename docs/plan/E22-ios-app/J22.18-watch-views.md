# J22.18 Apple Watch views in the app and the panel

Epic: [E22 Vitamux iOS app](README.md) · Status: in progress (app done; panel views open) · Depends on: J22.6, J22.14, J22.17
Read: [ios-app#apple-watch](../../architecture/ios-app.md#apple-watch), [frontend#chart-grammar](../../architecture/frontend.md#chart-grammar)

**Objective:** The new Watch data is visible and explainable in both clients, without interpretation.

## Outputs
- **Grammar:** the new quantities need no view code; Explore picks them up from the catalogue. The shared grammar fixture gains them.
- **New views, in the app and in the panel:**
  - ECG: the classification Apple gave, as recorded, average HR, symptoms, and the waveform strip at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV, scrollable, with a table fallback. No interpretation and no colouring by result.
  - Beat-to-beat: RR intervals over time per series, opened from an HRV reading.
  - Activity rings: move, exercise and stand per day as value against Apple's goal, with the goals labelled as Apple's.
  - Workout route: a map in the app (MapKit) and a plain path in the panel (no third-party tiles), with the workout's laps and activities.
  - Events lanes gain the new event families (rhythm alerts, mind, cycle tracking, symptoms).
- **Apple Watch card** on the app's Apple Health screen: groups turned on, and the types the Watch contributed with their last sample time (`GET /inventory` filtered by device).
- **Settings:** the new groups with plain explanations of what each one sends, sensitive ones marked.

## Done when
- UI tests (app) and Playwright specs (panel) on synthetic data for each view, including empty states.
- Copy review: nothing rates an ECG, rhythm or cycle value.
- No serious accessibility issues in either client.

## Notes
- **App** (`apple/VitamuxApp/Sources/Features/Specialised/Watch`): ECG list and recording (`vitamux://explore/ecg`, `…/ecg/{id}`; Apple's classification in HealthKit's words with its source, the strip on a paper grid at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV in 5-second tiles decimated once to two points per point column, a per-second table), beat-to-beat per series (`…/beats?date=`, from an HRV day's point sheet, the metric detail and Explore), activity rings as value-against-goal bars (`…/activity-rings`, from the four summary codes' detail), State of Mind (`…/state-of-mind`), and a workout's route on MapKit with its laps, intervals, activities, pauses and markers (`…/workouts/{id}`, from each workout row; at most 1,000 route points drawn). Events lanes and the type picker are ordered by family.
- **Apple Health screen** (`Features/AppleHealth`): the six registry v2 groups are listed apart as sensitive, each opening a plain sheet before iOS asks (the routes sheet names the Apple map tiles); routes are disabled until workouts are on; every group says what it sends. The Apple Watch card lists the groups on and the inventory items Apple Health sent from a watch, with the time of the newest record of that type (the inventory has no per-device time). `ThisDevice` now syncs the activity summary, resets and lists registry v2 anchors, and its gated store forwards the detail reads.
- **Fixtures:** `FakeServer+Watch.swift` (and `WatchFakeTests`); `fixtures/chart-grammar.json` gains `rr_interval`, `speed_xc_ski`, `apple_workout_effort` and `stand_hours`.
- **Tests:** `WatchUITests` covers each view with data and empty, the entries that open them, the opt-in sheets, the Watch card and a copy review; `DeepLinkUITests` lists the new links. Device checks (real HealthKit data, map tiles, VoiceOver on the strip) move to [J22.23](J22.23-device-campaign-release.md).
- **Open:** the panel's views (ECG strip, RR plot, rings, a tile-free route path, family lanes) and their Playwright specs.
