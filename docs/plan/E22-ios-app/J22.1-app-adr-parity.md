# J22.1 App ADR, parity matrix and screen map

Epic: [E22 Vitamux iOS app](README.md) · Status: todo · Depends on: None
Read: [ios-app](../../architecture/ios-app.md), [security#owner-sign-in](../../architecture/security.md#owner-sign-in), [connectors#oauth-connection-flow](../../architecture/connectors.md#oauth-connection-flow)

**Objective:** Settle every structural choice for the app before code, keep the architecture lean, and prove that each panel feature has a place on the phone.

## Outputs
- **ADR-0021** (`docs/adr/0021-ios-app.md`), recording:
  - native SwiftUI, Swift 6, iOS 18, iPhone only, no third-party SDKs;
  - the [lean architecture](../../architecture/ios-app.md#lean-architecture) rules: two packages (HealthBridgeKit unchanged, VitamuxKit with one library target), one app target with a folder per feature, `@Observable` models calling the client directly, one `AppState`, one `Route` enum, the allowed package list, and the layers that are ruled out;
  - `apple/VitamuxApp` replacing `apple/HealthBridgeApp`;
  - bearer app sessions: token format, lifetimes, Keychain class, revocation, why not cookies or API keys;
  - the native OAuth return (`/oauth/{provider}/start`, `vitamux://` callback) and why the scheme is fixed;
  - a client generated at build time from `api/openapi.yaml` through a symlink, nothing generated committed;
  - Swift Charts with a shared grammar fixture;
  - cache, widget and notification boundaries (local only, no APNs);
  - upgrade in place from Bridge (bundle id and Keychain service kept);
  - design after parity.
- [ios-app](../../architecture/ios-app.md): every *proposed* item settled; the [parity matrix](../../architecture/ios-app.md#parity-matrix) checked against `web/src/routes` and `api/authz.yaml`.
- **Skeleton:** the empty folder layout of `VitamuxKit` and `VitamuxApp` with one worked example (a screen, its model, its route, its UI test) that later jobs copy.
- **Screen map:** a short list per tab of screens, sheets and deep links (`vitamux://…`), with the panel route each one mirrors. Wireframe level only; no visual design.
- [security](../../architecture/security.md): the lost-phone threat row and the app-session line under owner sign-in.

## Done when
- The owner accepts ADR-0021.
- Every panel route and every owner endpoint the panel calls maps to a screen and a job, or is listed as out of scope with a reason.
