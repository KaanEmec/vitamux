# J26.3 Panel Day view

Epic: [E26 Intraday views on the server and the panel](README.md) · Status: done (2026-10-05) · Depends on: J26.2
Read: [frontend#chart-grammar](../../architecture/frontend.md#chart-grammar), [J22.26](../E22-ios-app/J22.26-intraday-views.md)

**Objective:** The metric page gets a Day range that zooms through the ladder.

## Outputs
- A `1D` range (already in the range picker) with a date stepper on every metric with `intraday`; zoom by drag or brush through the ladder; never finer than a source's native spacing.
- Overlays: night shading from the resolved sleep episode, workout bands, a "now" line on today, and the source strip when toggled on.
- The point panel shows value, bucket span, source and the explanation; a raw sample shows its timestamp, device and origin. "Show as a table" fallback.
- The all-sources day drilldown (`/explore/[metric]/day/[date]`) reuses the Day chart. Tapping a day on a longer range drills in.

## Done when
- Playwright covers the Day range, zoom levels and the table fallback; axe passes in both themes and at 375 px.
