# J23.8 Sleep, body and blood-pressure views

Epic: [E23 Charts and metric visualisation redesign](README.md) · Status: todo · Depends on: J23.4, J23.5
Read: [J21.9](../E21-visualisation/J21.9-specialised-views.md), [ADR-0009](../../adr/0009-sleep-date-night-window.md)

**Objective:** The canvas's specialised views.

## Outputs
- **Sleep:**
  - last night: a large hypnogram with a stage tooltip, plus duration, time in bed, efficiency, bedtime and wake;
  - nightly stage stacks with the mean;
  - the bedtime and wake chart, with the middle half of nights shaded;
  - the average night: stage ranges with the mean;
  - a nights list with stage bars and the source used.
- **Blood pressure:** a dumbbell per reading or session mean, a morning and evening filter, and a tooltip with pulse, posture and device.
- **Body:** weight readings with a 7-day moving average; body-composition tiles with sparklines and neutral deltas, and no class labels.
- Workouts and events restyled on the new tokens and lanes.

## Done when
- The Playwright specs cover each view in both themes and at 390 px.
