**Objective:** One code-owned source of truth for metrics and units.

## Outputs
- `internal/catalog` holding every `v1` code in [metric-catalog.md](../../architecture/metric-catalog.md): aggregation, base bucket, plausible range, allowed windows and strategies, and a provider-scoped flag for provider-namespaced scores
- Units and conversions
- Seed-migration generator and generated `docs/metrics.md`
- `metric-catalog.md` reduced to its rules and the codes not yet implemented

## Done when
- Seed and docs regenerate in CI with no diff.
- Every non-identity conversion is tested.
- SDNN and RMSSD are distinct and cannot share a rule.
- Every `v1` row of metric-catalog.md is in the catalogue with its unit, kinds and aggregation.
- Open questions 2–4 in metric-catalog.md are decided and recorded there.
