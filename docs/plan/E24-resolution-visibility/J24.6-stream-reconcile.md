# J24.6 Stream reconciliation and the owner's timezone for sidecars

Epic: [E24 Resolution defaults, opt-in gates and visible data](README.md) · Status: todo · Depends on: None
Read: [connectors#remote-sidecar-mode](../../architecture/connectors.md#remote-sidecar-mode), [connectors#runtime-responsibilities](../../architecture/connectors.md#runtime-responsibilities)

**Objective:** A connection's health reflects the streams its connector declares now, and sidecars cut days at the owner's local midnight.

Root cause seen in the field: a connection made while an old sidecar image still declared a retired stream (`whoop.steps`) drifted once on a manual sync. Retirement (`RetireStream`) only runs from a *scheduled* job of the gone stream; its incremental schedule was disabled and the correction one runs daily, so the degraded mark stayed and the connection showed `degraded`.

## Outputs
- On every describe refresh (`internal/connectors/remote/record.go`) and at startup, reconcile each connection of the provider: retire streams no longer declared (existing `RetireStream`), and ensure schedules for newly declared streams (`EnsureSchedules`, today only on connect or re-auth).
- A manual sync of an undeclared stream retires it too (`internal/connectors/runtime.go` requires a schedule id today).
- A stream whose schedules are all disabled by the owner does not keep the connection degraded (`RecordSyncSuccess`, `RetireStream` recomputation).
- The core sends the owner's timezone (from `timezone_periods`) with every sidecar fetch; the WHOOP sidecar's deep-dive day bounds use it (`sidecars/whoop/src/whoop.js`), and the step row is keyed by local date ([J25.4](../E25-catalogue-mappings/J25.4-whoop.md)).
- WHOOP workout list: use the official `start`/`end` filter if the private token honours it (otherwise keep the walk, now tolerant of a null `sport_id`).

## Done when
- Integration tests: a describe without a stream retires it and clears degraded; a new stream gets schedules; an owner-disabled degraded stream leaves the connection `active`.
- Sidecar test: a non-UTC timezone yields local-midnight bounds.
