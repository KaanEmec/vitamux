- Sleep-family resolution: one `metric: sleep` rule selects the episode, and all `sleep_*` codes read from it ([resolution#group-coherent-selection](../../architecture/resolution.md#group-coherent-selection))

## Done when
- Scenarios: matched night, partial capture excluded, split night, nap, a source without stages (stage codes give `no_stage_data`, not another source's stages), overlapping workouts from two providers.
