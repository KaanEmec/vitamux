# J09.11 Brand and device choices in rules, defaults rebuilt on them

Epic: [E09 Resolution engine](README.md) · Status: in progress · Depends on: J09.3, J09.2, [J20.7](../E20-guided-setup/J20.7-devices.md)
Read: [resolution#selectors-and-validation](../../architecture/resolution.md#selectors-and-validation), [resolution-defaults#how-brands-are-selected](../../architecture/resolution-defaults.md#how-brands-are-selected)

**Objective:** Rules pick a brand, an Apple Watch or iPhone, or one named device, as clear choices; the built-in defaults use the same named groups.

## Outputs
- Selector field `device_manufacturer` (optional in `vitamux.rule/1`, ADR-0008 allows additions), matched case-insensitively against `devices.manufacturer`. A row without a device never matches.
- The source queries carry the manufacturer; migration `00031` clears the resolved cache when a device's manufacturer changes.
- Built-ins (a new version per changed metric):
  - `apple_watch` and `iphone` by Apple's manufacturer and model;
  - `iphone` before `phone` in steps, distance and active energy;
  - relay groups for WHOOP, Polar and Fitbit;
  - Garmin and Withings relay groups also take those brands' devices in Apple Health.
- Rule builder: a "Choose a source or device" select built from `GET /source-devices` and `GET /providers` (Apple Health, Apple Watch, iPhone, each brand, each model, each named device; merged devices left out), and friendly labels for the built-in group ids.

## Done when
- Unit tests: brand matching is case-insensitive, brand plus model matches only that model, a source without a device never matches, `relayed: false` excludes relays, the length limit holds and the schema accepts the field.
- On fixturegen data: an Apple Watch rule takes only Watch rows, steps fall back from the watch to the iPhone, `whoop_apple` fills hours without direct WHOOP data, and a manufacturer change clears the cache.
- e2e: the picker offers the owner's choices, leaves out merged devices, writes manufacturer and model for Apple Watch, and the review step and rule lens show "Apple Watch".

Notes: the owner's WHOOP-before-Garmin heart-rate preference stays a personal rule. Garmin body-composition and BP rows carry no device yet ([J18.4](../E18-garmin/J18.4-normalizers.md) T18.4.6). Brand ranks are unchanged; only how a brand is selected changed.
